# Plan Content Calendar

Plan a week or month of LinkedIn posts — with specific topics, formats, days, and a strategic rationale.
Not filler. Not random. A real plan with logic behind what runs when.

## When to use
When Victor wants to plan ahead and batch-create content.

## What you do

1. Load `linkedin/config/profile.json`
2. Ask if not provided:
   - Time horizon (1 week | 2 weeks | 1 month)
   - Any specific events, themes, or campaigns to anchor to
   - Target posting frequency (default: 3-4x per week)
3. Design a calendar that:
   - Mixes formats across the week (don't run two data_insight posts back to back)
   - Balances pillars (don't over-index on career content vs. metrics content)
   - Uses high-engagement formats (hot_take, data_insight) on high-traffic days: Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday
   - Reserves weekend slots for lower-effort reflective posts or skips them
4. Present as a table with: Date | Day | Format | Topic | Hook Draft | Pillar

## Strategic rules
- Monday: avoid posting (low engagement day) — or use it for a "week in finance" soft post
- Tue/Wed/Thu: highest engagement — save best content for these days
- Friday: shorter posts, lighter tone
- Never post twice in 24 hours
- Every week should have at least one post that could generate debate (hot_take or counterintuitive data_insight)

## Content mix targets (per week at 3-4 posts):
- 1-2 metrics/technical posts (data_insight, framework_breakdown)
- 1 career/human post (career_reflection, lesson_learned)
- 1 opinion/debate post (hot_take)
- Optional: 1 list post or carousel

## After planning
Show the full calendar, then ask: "Want me to start writing any of these? I'd suggest starting with [specific post] because [reason]."
If any topic looks weak or too similar to the previous week, call it out directly.
